# Exploratory results — October 26, 2026

10 seeds × 5 variants = **50 fresh-process runs**. 128 cells; 160 steps; resource shift at step 80. These are development data, not a confirmatory study.

| Variant | Survival mean | Survival population SD | Wall seconds mean | Peak RSS MiB mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| random | 85.7031% | 8.0817% | 0.1133 | 26.03 |
| frozen | 85.3906% | 8.1942% | 0.1099 | 26.01 |
| greedy | 100.0000% | 0.0000% | 0.1185 | 26.03 |
| learned | 92.7344% | 6.0318% | 0.1236 | 26.05 |
| learned_no_communication | 92.8125% | 5.6747% | 0.0439 | 25.34 |

Learning beat random and frozen survival in all ten paired seeds. The fixed local greedy policy achieved 100% survival in every seed, outperforming learning in all ten. Communication did not show a clear benefit: learned minus no-communication survival averaged −0.078125 percentage points (2 wins, 4 ties, 4 losses).

This rejects any claim of demonstrated superiority over a simple local rule in this setting. It does not establish that learning or communication cannot help in other tasks. The current task exposes useful gradients directly, making a fixed policy strong.

No language, agents, cell growth, memory retention, physical energy savings, or recovery from cell damage was evaluated. Simulated energy is not joules. Whole-worker RSS includes interpreter/imports; timing excludes startup. Equal requested horizons need not imply equal actual work. Pairwise neighbor discovery is O(N²). Hardware details, all raw runs, per-seed differences, configuration hashes and limitations are retained in the adjacent JSON.
